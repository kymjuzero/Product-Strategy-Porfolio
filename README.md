# Product Strategy Portfolio

This is a collection of product strategy and innovation case studies developed through international business case competitions with the **Laurier Case Team**!

These projects explore how to identify customer needs, evaluate market opportunities, design innovative product experiences, and develop commercially viable strategies. Each case involved analyzing real business challenges, developing recommendations, and presenting solutions under competitive time constraints.

## Featured Case Studies

### 01. CWD — Enterprise AI Innovation
**Focus:** AI Product Strategy · Enterprise Software · Product Adoption

**Challenge:** How can Croonwolter&dros, a Dutch engineering and infrastructure company, leverage AI to improve operations and build long-term competitiveness?

**Our Solution — InsightX**

Proposed an enterprise AI adoption strategy centered on Microsoft Dynamics 365 and an internal innovation platform called *INSIGHT Engine*.

Key recommendations:
- Create a centralized platform for employees to propose AI use cases.
- Prioritize opportunities based on feasibility, business impact, and strategic alignment.
- Embed AI engineers within business teams to prototype and test solutions.
- Develop a phased rollout strategy using employee feedback and measurable success criteria.

**Product Skills:** Enterprise AI strategy, feature prioritization, stakeholder management, product roadmapping, and change management.

### 02. GoHost — AI-Powered Event Marketplace
**Focus:** Marketplace Strategy · AI Product Design · Customer Experience

**Challenge:** How can GoHost scale its AI-powered event booking platform while attracting both customers and venue partners?

**Our Solution — The Triple-G Strategy**

Designed a product and growth strategy focused on inexperienced event hosts who need a simpler way to plan events.

Key recommendations:
- Identify and prioritize high-value customer segments.
- Introduce an AI concierge that recommends venues and vendors based on customer needs.
- Design a seamless search, compare, and booking experience with customizable event packages.
- Develop a two-sided go-to-market strategy to acquire event hosts and vendors.

**Product Skills:** Customer segmentation, AI feature design, user journeys, marketplace economics, monetization, and go-to-market strategy.

### 03. Plantaform — Consumer Wellness Innovation
**Focus:** Consumer Product Strategy · UX · Engagement & Retention

**Challenge:** How can Plantaform scale its indoor gardening technology into a differentiated consumer wellness product?

**Our Solution — Plantaform Aura**

Proposed an integrated wellness experience connecting indoor gardening, mindfulness, and personalized digital engagement.

Key recommendations:
- Identify young urban professionals as the primary target customer segment.
- Integrate mindfulness experiences through a proposed partnership with Calm.
- Introduce gamification, wellness streaks, and personalized lighting experiences.
- Design customer journeys and growth initiatives to strengthen engagement and retention.

**Product Skills:** Consumer research, product positioning, feature ideation, personalization, engagement strategy, and customer experience design.

## My Approach to Product Strategy

Across these cases, I focused on five core stages of product thinking:

1. **Understand the user:** Identify customer segments, pain points, and unmet needs.
2. **Define the opportunity:** Evaluate market dynamics, competitive positioning, and business constraints.
3. **Design the solution:** Develop product concepts, features, and customer experiences.
4. **Build the business case:** Evaluate monetization, feasibility, trade-offs, and projected impact.
5. **Plan execution:** Develop implementation roadmaps, go-to-market strategies, and success metrics.
